# Ask Taokeh skill

An installable skill that turns a general AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, or any MCP-capable
client) into a competent day-to-day bookkeeper for a Malaysian SME whose books live in
[Taokeh](https://taokeh.my).

## What it does

The Ask Taokeh connector (Taokeh's MCP door) gives your AI a toolkit over your own books — reading
reports, drafting invoices and expenses, reviewing bank statements. The tools describe themselves, but an AI
meeting them cold still has to figure out the craft: which tool first, what the conventions are,
where the boundaries sit. This skill is that craft, written down:

- the money-in and money-out workflows, in the right order
- the weekly bank-review rhythm, and working the bank reconciliation with you
- how to read the business honestly (and how to say what the numbers mean)
- the ground rules: your AI drafts, **you** approve — always, in-app

## What you need

1. A company on Taokeh — the 14-day free trial includes the Ask Taokeh connector.
2. The connector linked to your AI app (in Taokeh: **Integrations → Ask Taokeh connector**).
3. This skill installed in your AI client.

Without a company on Taokeh the skill is just a readable description of how AI-first bookkeeping
works — the tools it drives only exist once your books are connected.

## What it will never do

File anything without a human approving it in Taokeh, accept terms on your behalf, buy or switch
on features, invent data you didn't provide, or give tax-interpretation advice. Those boundaries
are enforced by Taokeh's server, and the skill teaches your AI to respect them rather than fight
them.

## Not the same thing as Taokeh Bookkeeping

**Taokeh Bookkeeping** is a separate, paid, done-for-you service: human bookkeepers keep your
books for a fixed monthly fee. This skill is the do-it-yourself lane — your own AI, your own
hands on the approvals. If you'd rather hand the books over entirely, see
https://taokeh.my/bookkeeping.
